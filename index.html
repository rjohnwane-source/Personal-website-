My Favorite Things

These are some of the things I enjoy the most.

Food

I enjoy eating delicious food with my friends and family. One of my favorite drinks is milk tea. Sometimes, my friends and I hang out every Saturday night to drink milk tea and eat takoyaki while talking and enjoying our time together. My favorite dish is chicken curry, but I also enjoy Bicol Express, especially when it is spicy. I love trying different foods and sharing meals with the people I care about.

Harry Potter

Harry Potter is one of my favorite movie series. I enjoy watching its magical and exciting story, especially Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban. I love the plot of the movie, especially the concept of time travel and how it plays an important role in the story. The movie is fascinating because of its mystery, adventure, and magical it is.
